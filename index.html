FAQ

Questions before you start.

Is VivaPantry only a recipe app?

No. Recipes are one part of a connected system for pantry, meal planning, grocery lists, receipts, and household preferences.

Does VivaPantry give medical or nutrition advice?

No. VivaPantry provides planning support only. Review ingredients, allergens, labels, and health decisions yourself.

How does receipt scanning help?

Receipt data can help update pantry records, purchase history, and price context when the feature is available in the app.

Is premium for one person or a household?

Premium is designed around household access and shared planning where supported by the app.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Visit the Delete Account page or contact support with your app account email.
