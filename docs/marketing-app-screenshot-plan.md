# VivaPantry marketing app screenshot plan

Use only screenshots captured from the real Android app. Do not create mock screens, composite fake data, or AI-generated UI.

## Capture setup

- Use the version 93 production build.
- Use a clean demo household with believable, non-sensitive data.
- Capture in English first; repeat the final selected set in Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish only if localized landing pages will show localized screens.
- Use the same Android device, theme, font scale, and system-bar treatment throughout.
- Prefer portrait screenshots at the phone's native resolution. Keep the full app frame; crop consistently during website production.
- Remove personal email addresses, precise locations, household invite codes, real receipts, and notification identifiers before capture.

## Required screenshots

1. **Home: the weekly decision at a glance**
   - Show the signed-in Home screen with a useful weekly summary and clear next action.
   - Marketing job: establish the product as a connected household system, not another recipe catalog.

2. **Meal plan: a practical week already planned**
   - Show a populated weekly meal plan with several days and recognizable meal cards.
   - Marketing job: prove the core promise, “know what to cook.”

3. **Shop: the focused grocery list**
   - Show grouped shopping items, a selected store if available, and a meaningful mix of checked and unchecked items.
   - Marketing job: connect planned meals and pantry gaps to “know what to buy.”

4. **Pantry: what is already at home**
   - Show a populated pantry with quantities, units, categories, and at least one low-stock item.
   - Marketing job: prove the system starts with existing food rather than a blank list.

5. **Receipt or price capture: close the loop**
   - Show the real receipt review or price-history result after a successful scan; avoid displaying a real merchant receipt containing personal data.
   - Marketing job: demonstrate how shopping updates the household's records for the next cycle.

## Optional supporting screenshots

- Recipe detail with pantry availability context.
- Household preferences that influence planning.
- Cook Today showing the immediate meal decision.
- Price history for one ordinary grocery item.

## Website placement

- Hero: use the Home screen only after it is captured; until then retain the honest Plan → Shop → Store → Cook → Repeat diagram.
- Product story: place Meal plan, Shop, and Pantry in a three-step visual sequence.
- Proof of loop: place Receipt/price capture beside the explanation of how purchase history improves the next plan.
- Use one screen per claim. Do not place unrelated screens beside marketing copy that implies a capability they do not show.
