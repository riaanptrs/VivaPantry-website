# VivaPantry marketing site screenshot wishlist

This document lists the screenshots and polished mockups to capture later for the public marketing website. The current redesign intentionally removes the existing app screenshots until the app UI is ready for curated marketing use.

## Capture standards

- Use clean demo data only.
- Do not show private household data, real receipts, real addresses, personal emails, API keys, tokens, or payment details.
- Capture English and Brazilian Portuguese first. Capture Spanish when the Spanish app UI is release-ready.
- Prefer a modern Android device frame or clean cropped screenshots, but do not distort the real UI.
- Use consistent device size, status-bar treatment, and background treatment across all screenshots.
- Avoid showing unfinished UI, debug text, test accounts, placeholder translations, or cluttered states.

## 1. Home / Decision Hub

- **Purpose on website:** Primary hero or major feature proof point. Show that VivaPantry helps users decide what to cook next and what is missing.
- **Suggested composition:** Clean home state with next meal, pantry-aware prompt, missing ingredients, and one clear next action.
- **Locale needed:** en, pt-BR; es later.
- **Device recommendation:** Samsung S24 Ultra or similar Android portrait screenshot; also prepare a cropped web-friendly 9:16 version.
- **Screenshot type:** Real screenshot or polished mockup based on real UI.
- **Privacy/data notes:** Use demo household, demo meal, no personal account data.

## 2. Pantry Overview

- **Purpose on website:** Prove the app tracks what the household already has.
- **Suggested composition:** Pantry categories, quantities, units, and low-stock state.
- **Locale needed:** en, pt-BR; es later.
- **Device recommendation:** Android portrait.
- **Screenshot type:** Real screenshot preferred.
- **Privacy/data notes:** Use generic grocery items and realistic quantities.

## 3. Meal Plan Week View

- **Purpose on website:** Explain weekly planning and show the Plan part of the loop.
- **Suggested composition:** A realistic week with meals assigned to days and meal slots supported by the app.
- **Locale needed:** en, pt-BR; es later.
- **Device recommendation:** Android portrait; consider a wider cropped version if the UI supports it.
- **Screenshot type:** Real screenshot or polished mockup.
- **Privacy/data notes:** Avoid sensitive dietary or health information.

## 4. Grocery List / Shopping Mode

- **Purpose on website:** Show focused shopping from meal plans and pantry gaps.
- **Suggested composition:** Shopping list with pending and bought states, filters, and no visual clutter.
- **Locale needed:** en, pt-BR; es later.
- **Device recommendation:** Android portrait.
- **Screenshot type:** Real screenshot preferred after the shopping-mode UI is finalized.
- **Privacy/data notes:** Use demo supermarket/product data only.

## 5. Receipt Scan Result

- **Purpose on website:** Explain the value of receipt scanning for pantry updates and price history.
- **Suggested composition:** Parsed receipt result with product names, quantities, and price observations.
- **Locale needed:** en, pt-BR; es later.
- **Device recommendation:** Android portrait.
- **Screenshot type:** Real screenshot with demo receipt data.
- **Privacy/data notes:** Do not show real receipt numbers, store addresses, payment method, loyalty number, or personal purchase data.

## 6. Recipe Detail

- **Purpose on website:** Show structured ingredients and cooking instructions.
- **Suggested composition:** Attractive recipe detail with clear ingredients and steps.
- **Locale needed:** en, pt-BR; es later.
- **Device recommendation:** Android portrait.
- **Screenshot type:** Real screenshot or polished mockup.
- **Privacy/data notes:** Use owned/demo recipe text and images only. Do not use copyrighted recipe content unless licensed or owned.

## 7. Household Preferences / Onboarding Summary

- **Purpose on website:** Show personalization and household-based planning.
- **Suggested composition:** Summary of household size, cooking time, budget preference, meal goals, and regional settings.
- **Locale needed:** en, pt-BR; es later.
- **Device recommendation:** Android portrait.
- **Screenshot type:** Cropped real screenshot or polished mockup.
- **Privacy/data notes:** Avoid sensitive dietary, medical, allergy, or child-specific information.

## 8. Premium / Upgrade Screen

- **Purpose on website:** Support pricing page once final store terms are confirmed.
- **Suggested composition:** Clear Free vs Premium comparison and household-based premium wording.
- **Locale needed:** en, pt-BR; es later.
- **Device recommendation:** Android portrait.
- **Screenshot type:** Real screenshot only after final pricing, trial, renewal, cancellation, and refund wording are confirmed.
- **Privacy/data notes:** Do not show real billing account, payment method, or purchase tokens.

## 9. Trust / Privacy / Account Deletion Screen

- **Purpose on website:** Reinforce privacy and compliance confidence.
- **Suggested composition:** Account settings, privacy options, support route, or delete-account path.
- **Locale needed:** en, pt-BR; es later.
- **Device recommendation:** Android portrait.
- **Screenshot type:** Real screenshot or cropped screenshot.
- **Privacy/data notes:** Hide personal email, account IDs, provider connections, and private storage data.

## Follow-up design notes

- Capture screenshots only after the relevant app screens are visually stable.
- Prefer fewer, stronger screenshots over many mediocre ones.
- Each screenshot should support one marketing claim.
- Recheck legal/privacy copy before using screenshots that show AI, receipts, pricing, or account deletion.
