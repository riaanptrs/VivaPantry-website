  2. Plan meals around your household.

FAQ

Questions before you start

Is VivaPantry only a recipe app?

No. Recipes are one part of a larger Plan, Shop, Store, Cook, Repeat system for household food planning.

Does VivaPantry give medical or nutrition advice?

No. VivaPantry does not provide medical, nutritional, allergy, or diet advice. Review all suggestions before using them.

How does receipt scanning help?

Receipts can help update what came home, preserve purchase history, and build a clearer price record over time.

Is premium for one person or a household?

Premium is designed around household planning where appropriate, including shared preferences, routines, and weekly coordination.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. The Delete Account page explains how to request deletion and what data categories are covered.
