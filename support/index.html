Support

VivaPantry Support

For VivaPantry support, email support@vivapantry.com and include the account email you use in the app.

Contact

Email support@vivapantry.com for help with the app, your account, or data deletion requests.

Login help

If you cannot sign in, include the email address used for your VivaPantry account and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords.

Receipts

For receipt scanning issues, describe the store, the receipt format, and whether the problem was image upload, item extraction, or price matching.

Meal plans

For meal plan issues, tell us whether the plan was generated by AI or created manually, and what result you expected.

Grocery lists

For grocery list issues, mention whether the item came from a recipe, meal plan, receipt, or manual entry.

Google and third-party services

For Google Maps, Google Drive, Google Play, or other third-party issues, describe the feature you used, the approximate time, the store/link/account involved if relevant, and any visible error message. Do not send passwords, API keys, access tokens, or full payment details.

Account deletion

You can request deletion from the Delete Account page or by emailing support with your app account email.
