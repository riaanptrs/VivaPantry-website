Delete Account

Delete your VivaPantry account

You can request deletion of your VivaPantry account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

1. Send an email to support@vivapantry.com with the subject line "Delete my account".
2. Include the email address used to create your VivaPantry account.
3. Our team will confirm receipt and process the request.

Data that will be deleted

The deletion request covers these categories:

  • Account information
  • Profile and preference data
  • Pantry items
  • Shopping lists
  • Recipes created or saved
  • Meal plans
  • Data associated with use of the app

Data that may be temporarily retained

Some technical records, security logs, anti-fraud records, or information required for legal obligations may be retained for a limited period when necessary.

Processing time

Deletion requests will be processed within 30 days after the request is confirmed.

Questions

If you have questions, contact support@vivapantry.com.
